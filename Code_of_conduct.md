# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Desktop-AI project and its community will be treated with respect and dignity, regardless of their identity or experience level.

We value:

- 🤝 **Inclusivity** — Everyone is welcome
- 💡 **Collaboration** — We work together
- 🎯 **Respect** — Different perspectives are valued
- 🛡️ **Safety** — A harassment-free environment
- 🌱 **Growth** — Learning and improvement

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- ✅ Using welcoming and inclusive language
- ✅ Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- ✅ Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- ✅ Focusing on what is best for the community
- ✅ Showing empathy towards other community members
- ✅ Helping others learn and grow
- ✅ Acknowledging and celebrating contributions
- ✅ Admitting mistakes and learning from them

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- ❌ Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- ❌ Personal attacks or derogatory comments
- ❌ Trolling, insulting, or derogatory language
- ❌ Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention
- ❌ Publishing private information without consent
- ❌ Violent threats or violent language
- ❌ Deliberate intimidation or stalking
- ❌ Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies:

- **In all project spaces:** GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, commits
- **In community spaces:** Mailing lists, chat channels, forums
- **In public spaces:** When representing the project or community (conferences, social media, etc.)

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you encounter unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **Emailing:** gokul3krish2@gmail.com
2. **GitHub:** Report to project maintainers
3. **Provide:** Details of the incident and any evidence

### Response to Reports

- All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly
- Confidentiality of reporters will be maintained
- Reporters will not face retaliation
- Appropriate action will be taken based on findings

### Consequences

Violations may result in:

1. **Warning** — Clear explanation of violation
2. **Temporary Ban** — Removal from community activities (1-30 days)
3. **Permanent Ban** — Permanent removal from the community
4. **Escalation** — Involvement of law enforcement if necessary

The severity of consequences matches the severity of the violation.

---

## Appeals

If you believe action taken was unjustified, you may appeal by:

1. Emailing details to: gokul3krish2@gmail.com
2. Providing new evidence or context
3. Awaiting review and response (within 7 days)
